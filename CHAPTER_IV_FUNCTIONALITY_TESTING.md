## 4.3 System Usability Scale (SUS) Results

The System Usability Scale (SUS) was administered to evaluate the usability of the ARrange Application. The SUS questionnaire was distributed to both **buyers and sellers** who actively used the system during the evaluation period.

### **4.3.1 SUS Score Calculation**

The SUS scores were calculated using the standard formula:
- **SUS Score = (Sum of adjusted scores) × 2.5**
- **Range**: 0-100
- **Interpretation**: 
  - 80-100: Excellent
  - 68-79: Good
  - 51-67: OK
  - 0-50: Poor

### **4.3.2 User Demographics**

| User Type | Number of Participants | Average SUS Score | Usability Rating |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Buyers** | 25 | 87.2 | Excellent |
| **Sellers** | 15 | 84.6 | Excellent |
| **Overall** | 40 | 86.1 | Excellent |
